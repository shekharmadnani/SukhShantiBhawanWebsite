Practicalities

How enrolling works

  1. 01

    Call, write, or simply arrive

    An evening meditation is the easiest first contact. If you would rather arrange things beforehand, call {{ site.contact.phone }} or write to {{ site.contact.email }}.

  2. 02

    A teacher is set aside for you

    The foundation course is usually taught one to one, at an hour that fits around your work. Small groups run when several people start together.

  3. 03

    Seven sessions, at your pace

    Daily, on alternate days, or weekly. Missing one is not a problem; the sessions are picked up where they were left.

  4. 04

    Afterwards, nothing is required

    Some people join the morning class. Some come only on Sundays. Some take what they have learned and are never seen again — which is a perfectly good outcome.
