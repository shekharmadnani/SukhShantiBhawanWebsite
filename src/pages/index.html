Welcome

Peace is not somewhere else

Most people arrive here carrying something — a job that will not let go, a loss, a restlessness they cannot name. They expect to be given a belief. Instead they are given ten minutes of silence, and their own mind back.

Sukh Shanti Bhawan — the house of happiness and peace — is one of more than eight thousand Brahma Kumaris centres around the world. What is taught here is not a religion to join. It is an understanding of the self, and a meditation practised with the eyes open, so that it can be carried into an ordinary Tuesday.

The centre is run entirely by its own students. Nothing is sold, no fee is taken, and no one will ask you to become anything you are not.

Rajyoga meditation

Meditation with your eyes open

Rajyoga asks for no posture, no chanting and no incense. You sit as you are, keep your eyes gently open, and turn your attention to the one who is looking. That is the whole technique. Everything else is practice.

  1. 01

    You are not your thoughts

    The first shift is noticing that something is watching the thinking. That something is you.

  2. 02

    Peace is the original state

    It is not produced by the practice. It is what remains when the noise is set down.

  3. 03

    A relationship, not a ritual

    Attention turns towards the Supreme — experienced as light, and as a relationship that asks nothing.

  4. 04

    Carried into the day

    Because the eyes stay open, the state survives the commute, the meeting and the argument.
