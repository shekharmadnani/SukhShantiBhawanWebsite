Why wings

The same understanding, in different rooms

What helps a surgeon steady themselves before an operation is not phrased the same way as what helps a sixteen-year-old before an exam.

The Brahma Kumaris organise outward-facing work into wings — groups of students who already work in a field and can speak to it without translation. A doctor talks to doctors. A teacher talks to teachers. The understanding underneath is identical; only the vocabulary changes.

Every session listed below is offered free. If you would like one for your school, hospital, office, RWA or society anywhere in Noida, write to us and someone will call you back.
